Suikai Privacy Policy

Effective version: 1.0. This policy explains how Suikai handles information when you use its marketplace app and related public pages.

Information you provide

We process account and profile information, including name, email address when you use email sign-up, phone number, Viber contact number, profile photo, city and optional location. We process the shops, listings, descriptions, prices, photos, videos, reports, and other content you submit. A Telegram sign-in may provide Telegram identity details such as an identifier, name, username, picture, and, if Telegram supplies it, phone number.

Information created through use

Suikai processes listing views, likes, reports, blocks, in-app notification data and read state, and a locally generated random device/application identifier. Server-side hashes of that identifier are used for interaction counting, rate limiting, anti-spam, and abuse prevention. If you permit location access, we process precise location to show nearby content, choose a listing/shop location, and display map information. City information may be used when precise location is unavailable.

How information is used

We use information to operate accounts and marketplace features; publish and display listings and shops; enable seller/customer contact; provide maps and location-based functionality; protect the service; investigate reports; enforce our Terms and Community Guidelines; manage notifications; and improve reliability and security where supported by the service.

Public information and sharing

Listings and shops can make submitted names, descriptions, prices, photos, videos, city, and contact details public. Location coordinates are displayed only when the owner enables location visibility. We share information with service providers that operate the service, including Supabase for authentication, database, storage and server functions. We may disclose information where reasonably necessary for security, fraud prevention, enforcement, or lawful requests.

When you choose Telegram sign-in, Telegram processes the sign-in flow. TikTok receives requests when you play embedded TikTok content. OpenStreetMap receives map-tile requests. Opening Viber, a phone link, Google Maps/directions, or another external link sends you to that provider; their privacy practices apply. Suikai requests a THB exchange rate from ExchangeRate-API without sending account fields in that request.

Retention and deletion

We retain account information and content while needed to operate Suikai and as needed for security, fraud prevention, legal obligations, and dispute handling. You can delete your account in the app at Profile → Edit profile → Delete account, or use our public account deletion page. The deletion process deletes your account, profile, owned shops/listings/media, and account-linked interactions and requests. De-identified view/security information and information required for legal or security reasons may be retained only as necessary.

Security and children

We use reasonable technical and organizational safeguards, including secure production connections and account-based access controls, but no service can guarantee absolute security. Suikai is a general marketplace and is not directed to children. Do not use it where you are not legally able to consent to this policy or use marketplace services.

Changes and contact

We may update this policy and will publish the current version. Contact: suikai.app@gmail.com
