Suikai Community Guidelines

Suikai is a marketplace. Keep it safe, lawful, accurate, and respectful.

Do not post, sell, offer, or promote

  • Illegal goods or services, stolen goods, prohibited weapons, drugs, or illegal substances.
  • Scams, fraud, deceptive payment/contact requests, impersonation, spam, malware, phishing, or deceptive links.
  • Sexual or exploitative content, including any child sexual abuse or exploitation material. Report this immediately; it is never permitted.
  • Hate, violent extremist content, credible threats, harassment, bullying, or content encouraging violence.
  • Another person’s private or personal information without permission.
  • Copyright, trademark, or other intellectual-property infringement.
  • Misleading listings, false prices, inaccurate photos/videos, or goods you are not authorized to offer.

Report, block, and moderation

Use Report for violating listings, shops, or users. Use Block to stop seeing a seller. Suikai may investigate reports, remove or hide content, suspend shops/accounts, and act more strongly for repeated violations. We may preserve or disclose information where necessary to meet lawful requests or protect people and the service.

Be accountable

Use your own accurate content and information. Treat other users respectfully. Buyers and sellers should use caution and lawful arrangements when contacting each other or completing offline transactions.
